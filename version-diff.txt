Carrier route fixes, neutron auto-plot, HUD colours, mic diagnostics

---

- **Fleet carrier routes no longer come back from the dead.** EliteIntel used to re-plot a carrier route by itself whenever the carrier turned up somewhere the plotted route did not mention, and it read the destination out of the very route it was replacing. That made an abandoned route self-regenerating: clearing it only held until the next carrier jump or the next app start. One commander carried a one-leg route for weeks that survived both the clear command and a manual delete. Arriving off-route now simply voids the route, and the calculate command is the only thing in the app that ever plots one.
- **Fixed the jump count spoken on carrier arrival.** A single jump the commander scheduled himself was reported as "one jump remaining", counting a leg of a stale route he was never flying, and with no route plotted at all it announced "final destination reached" because zero legs were left. The arrival now reports what actually happened to the voyage, and says nothing about legs when there is no voyage.
- **Added an announcement when a route is dropped.** An off-route arrival now states once that the plotted route no longer applies and has been cleared, rather than the voyage disappearing in silence. Translated into all nine languages.
- **Fixed a carrier parked on the last leg of its route reading as having no route at all**, which left dead legs behind to reappear as leg 1 the next time it moved.
- **Startup is quicker and fully offline.** The last network call was removed from the journal scan EliteIntel runs when it starts, so it now reaches nothing at all.

- **Added an opt-in neutron highway assist.** Supercharging in a jet cone now announces the next waypoint, waits ten seconds so you can fly clear of the cone, and then plots the course for you. Flying a neutron route was three moves at every waypoint - arrive, take the boost, ask for a course - and this removes the third. It is off by default, under Ship Options on the Commander tab, because a galaxy map that opens unasked is alarming and the game journal cannot tell whether a boost was taken to follow the route or for a jump of your own.
- The plot is re-checked after the ten second wait rather than fired blindly, so switching the option off, jumping away or leaving the ship during the countdown cancels it. If the route plotter has nothing to do (already on course, or nowhere to go) it now says so instead of leaving you watching for a map that never opens.
- The callout naming the destination and the jet cone was added in all nine languages.

- **The music playlist now loops.** Playing in order used to stop dead after the last track; it now rolls round to the first one, both when a track ends on its own and when you ask for the next track on the last row. Random order was already endless and is unchanged. Asking for the previous track at the top of the list deliberately does not roll backwards.
- **Added a "play the playlist from the top" voice command** ("restart the playlist", "back to the first track" and equivalents), in all nine languages, which starts the first track from the beginning rather than resuming where you left off.
- A playlist pointing at missing files still gives up after one pass rather than spinning forever.

- **Made the HUD overlay text colours yours to choose.** The objective title, the good, warning and critical row states, the labels, and the three conversation lanes (your words, the ship AI, radio traffic) can each be recoloured from the HUD overlay settings, with one button that puts the whole palette back to the shipped colours. Picks apply live.
- Only the colours you actually change are stored, so a colour retuned in a later release still reaches anyone who never overrode it, and the reset simply forgets your choices. The card background is deliberately not on the palette: the transparency slider is already the control over it, and a colour picker as well would let a card be made bright and opaque enough to hide the cockpit behind it.
- **Fixed two dark-theme faults that the new colour picker exposed**: a selected tab drawn white on white, and a black-on-dark heading around the colour chooser's preview panel. The colour chooser now opens dressed for the dark theme wherever it is used, including the three input monitor dialogs.
- Rebuilt the Linux and Windows overlay binaries with the new colour support.

- **Added a microphone report to the support bundle.** "The app does not hear me" was the one report the bundle could not help with, because nothing about the microphone reaches the application log and every cause looks identical from the commander's chair. The bundle now carries a mic-diagnostics.txt that opens with a verdict naming the earliest stage that is failing, then reports the input device, the levels, the voice gate and push-to-talk in that order.
- It also calls out the two failures that are invisible in the numbers: a saved input device that no longer exists (which silently falls back to the system default) and a push-to-talk controller that is not plugged in, named against the controllers actually present.
- **No audio is ever recorded.** Only loudness readings are taken off each frame, and the frame is dropped immediately. Nothing you say is stored or attached, and the whole summary is a few hundred bytes.

- **Fixed the same scanned target being announced over and over.** Killing any target used to wipe the record of every contact already announced, so re-acquiring an unrelated ship you had already been told about announced it again. Only the destroyed target's record is now cleared. A target you scanned before this update may be announced once more before its record is rebuilt.
- A contact that cannot be identified from the journal is still announced, on the grounds that hearing the same contact twice costs a repeated sentence while missing it once costs the contact.

- **Stopped the binding conflict report flagging one lighting control that wears different names in different contexts.** The ship spot light, the SRV headlights and the on-foot torch are one control to the commander, and so are vehicle and on-foot night vision, so putting each of them on a single key is a deliberate layout, not a fault. Reporting it taught commanders to skip the whole conflict report.
- A lamp sharing a key with night vision is still reported, because those are two separate controls and EliteIntel taps both itself when it turns lights and night vision off for a jump. Every other overlap is reported exactly as before. The bindings editor accepts the same layouts the report now allows, so the SRV lamp can be put on the key the ship lamp already holds.

- **Re-enabled the progress beep while a background Spansh search is running**, so a long route or trade search no longer sounds like nothing is happening.

- **Removed the "Request fighter dock on FTL / cancel if out" option.** The automation has been broken since the Nomad update and the checkbox was already permanently disabled, so it and its label were taken out of all nine languages.
